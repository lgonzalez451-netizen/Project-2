What is PixelPlay?

PixelPlay is a website where you can learn about pixel-related games. There are many games now that are high quality with fantastic 3D graphics, but we are forgetting that the simplest graphics can still be fun. Here you will learn the game’s quick summary, where you can play, and additional information. Have fun learning and exploring the wide selection of pixel-related games.
